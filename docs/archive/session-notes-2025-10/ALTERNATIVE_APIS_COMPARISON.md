# 🔄 Alternative Football APIs - Comparison

## 📋 Overview

After discovering the severe limitations of TheSportsDB Free Tier, here are alternative APIs to consider.

---

## 1️⃣ **Football-Data.org**

### 📊 Details:
- **Website**: https://www.football-data.org/
- **Free Tier**: ✅ Yes
- **Current Season**: ✅ Yes (2024-2025)
- **Rate Limit**: 10 requests/minute
- **API Calls/Day**: Unlimited (with rate limit)

### ✅ **What's Included (Free Tier):**
- ✅ Current season data
- ✅ All major European leagues
- ✅ Full team rosters
- ✅ Full standings/tables
- ✅ All fixtures (past and future)
- ✅ Match scores
- ✅ Team statistics
- ✅ Player data (limited)

### ❌ **Limitations (Free Tier):**
- ⚠️ 10 requests/minute (slower than TheSportsDB)
- ⚠️ Limited to specific competitions
- ⚠️ No live scores
- ⚠️ Limited player statistics

### 💰 **Pricing:**
- **Free**: 10 requests/minute
- **Paid**: Contact for pricing

### 🎯 **Verdict:**
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **HIGHLY RECOMMENDED** - Best free option for your use case!

## 2️⃣ **API-Football (Current Provider)**

### 📊 Details:
- **Website**: https://www.api-football.com/
- **Free Tier**: ✅ Yes (via RapidAPI)
- **Current Season**: ❌ No (2021-2023 only)
- **Rate Limit**: 100 requests/day

### ✅ **What's Included (Free Tier):**
- ✅ Excellent data quality
- ✅ All teams and standings (for 2021-2023)
- ✅ All fixtures (for 2021-2023)
- ✅ Comprehensive statistics
- ✅ Good documentation

### ❌ **Limitations (Free Tier):**
- 🔴 **No current season** (2021-2023 only)
- ⚠️ 100 requests/day limit
- ⚠️ Requires RapidAPI authentication

### 💰 **Pricing:**
- **Free**: 100 requests/day (2021-2023 seasons)
- **Basic**: $10/month - 3,000 requests/day
- **Pro**: $25/month - 10,000 requests/day
- **Ultra**: $50/month - 30,000 requests/day

### 🎯 **Verdict:**
⭐⭐⭐ **GOOD** - Excellent quality but expensive for current season

---

## 3️⃣ **TheSportsDB**

### 📊 Details:
- **Website**: https://www.thesportsdb.com/
- **Free Tier**: ✅ Yes
- **Current Season**: ✅ Yes (2024-2025)
- **Rate Limit**: 30 requests/minute

### ✅ **What's Included (Free Tier):**
- ✅ Current season data
- ✅ League information
- ✅ Team details
- ✅ Team badges/logos
- ✅ Events by date (5 per day)

### ❌ **Limitations (Free Tier):**
- 🔴 **Only 10 teams per league** (need 20)
- 🔴 **Only 5 teams in standings** (need 20)
- 🔴 **Only 1 next/previous event** (need multiple)
- 🔴 **Only 15 matches per season** (need 380+)

### 💰 **Pricing:**
- **Free**: Severely limited
- **Premium**: $9/month - Much better limits + V2 API + Livescores

### 🎯 **Verdict:**
⭐⭐ **NOT RECOMMENDED (Free)** - Too limited for your needs
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **GOOD (Premium $9)** - Best value if you upgrade
